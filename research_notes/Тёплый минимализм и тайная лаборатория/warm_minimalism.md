## 1. Which games are known for warm, minimal, hand-painted art?

### Takeaway
Gris (watercolour look, colour as the story key) and Dorfromantik (written style guide, basic shapes, "simple but warm and playful") have the best art-direction statements. Townscaper and A Short Hike are sourced for their rule-based or limited-palette methods. Monument Valley is sourced for its Escher basis. Florence is an interactive story, not an art-direction source. None of the main titles was verified as a browser build. The only browser-candidate titles are small itch.io games whose details are unverified.

### Cited Findings

**Gris (Nomada Studio)**
- The art pairs computer graphics with traditional art. The team studied how to recreate watercolour and pencil shading so the game looks handcrafted — [A Watercolor Wonderland: Taking A Closer Look At GRIS](https://www.thegeeklygrind.com/archives/a-closer-look-at-gris) (snippet only; page not fetched; no date shown).
- Level design echoes watercolour pooling on paper, with colour splotches spilling past the lines — same source (snippet).
- Colour carries the story and tracks plot and characters. Producer Roger Mendoza said this to The Verge (The Verge URL not returned by search); a Nomada interview on colour as the most important element — [Vice interview with Nomada Studio](https://www.vice.com/en/article/nomada-studio-lets-me-in-to-learn-how-gris-and-neva-deliver-a-beautiful-and-touching-message-interview) and [PLITCH interview](https://blogs.plitch.com/en/blog/interview-with-nomada-studio) (snippet; which page holds which quote not confirmed).
- Chapters are organised around different colours. The team worked from a full storyboard, "like an animated feature" — [PLITCH interview](https://blogs.plitch.com/en/blog/interview-with-nomada-studio) (snippet).
- Concept art by Conrad Roset; senior artist Ari Cervelló Polo described translating his ideas into game art — [The Pixels: Interview with Conrad Roset](https://www.the-pixels.com/?p=109743) (snippet).
- For Neva, the team deliberately moved away from the watercolour look toward a wider palette and flat colour, keeping some watercolour resources. Source is a mirror site of unknown original — [rofluno.pages.dev mirror](https://rofluno.pages.dev/posts/neva-lead-producer-talks-making-its-art-style-changes-from-gris-and-more). Treat as unverified quote.
- Gris won a best-art-direction award among other honours (snippet). No award source URL verified.

**Dorfromantik (Toukana Interactive)**
- Minimalism was present from the start, partly for practical reasons. The early style guide set rules for proportions, lighting and perspective. The target is "simple but warm and playful," with most visuals built from very basic shapes, carefully composed — [80.lv: How Dorfromantik expands its cozy world through minimalist design](https://80.lv/articles/how-dorfromantik-expands-its-cozy-world-through-minimalist-design) (snippet; fetch blocked; no date shown).
- Influences conflict. Wikipedia lists landscape paintings and photography — [Wikipedia](https://www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorfromantik) (snippet). cavac.at links it to romanticist and impressionist painting — [cavac.at](https://cavac.at/cavacopedia/Dorfromantik). Neither was confirmed against a developer statement. Conflict noted, not resolved.
- Team: four members, each with a bachelor's in game design from HTW Berlin. German-language interview, 2022 — [Galaxus interview](https://galaxus.de/en/page/dorfromantik-developer-success-has-opened-a-lot-of-doors-for-us-23775).
- Review framing the game as "masterful minimalism" — [Mundo Gamer](https://mundogamer.community/en/articles/dorfromantiks-masterful-minimalism-will-soothe-your-soul) (listed, not read).

**Townscaper (Oskar Stålberg)**
- Described as low-poly with a simple, minimalist UI; press materials pitch a calming art style — [Wikipedia: Townscaper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townscaper) (listed, not fetched).
- Stålberg says flat-shaded styles and simple lighting help keep resource use low, and that making art work on weak hardware was among the biggest challenges (snippet; per-claim URL not confirmed).
- Worlds come from tile-based procedural generation over hand-designed building pieces, with adjacency rules deciding fit — [Game Developer: How Townscaper works, a story four games in the making](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/how-townscaper-works-a-story-four-games-in-the-making) (listed; snippet).
- Sold about 380,000 copies on Steam — [MCV/Develop: Townscaper lead](https://mcvuk.com/business-news/when-we-made-townscaper) (sales figure only; date not shown).
- Stålberg gave an IndieCade Europe 2019 talk on terrain and building generation (memory, unverified; no URL verified).

**Monument Valley (ustwo)**
- The early concept drew on M.C. Escher, and the final design did not deviate far from it — [Wikipedia: Monument Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_Valley_(video_game)) (listed; snippet). See also [Core77: "Get lost in Monument Valley, the MC Escher of iPad games"](https://www.core77.com/posts/26744/get-lost-in-monument-valley-the-mc-escher-of-ipad-games-26744).
- Credits per Wikipedia snippet: Neil McFarland (director); Ken Wong and David Fernández Huerta among the artists.
- Colour is used to cue the player indirectly (Wikipedia snippet).

**Florence (Mountains; Ken Wong as designer)**
- Presented as an "interactive story," not a game, with no branching choices — [Annapurna Interactive: Florence](https://annapurnainteractive.com/games/florence); [Stanford Daily, 2023-03-14](https://stanforddaily.com/2023/03/14/interactive-graphic-novel-florence-is-the-perfect-heartbreak-for-2-99/).
- Described as a wordless new form of a familiar story — [AV Club](https://avclub.com/florence-tells-a-familiar-story-in-a-wordless-new-form-1823616786).

**A Short Hike (Adam Robinson-Yu)**
- The art style was the first thing developed: a small autumn forest diorama using as few pixels as possible — [Nintendo Everything interview](https://nintendoeverything.com/interview-a-short-hike-dev-on-bringing-the-game-to-switch-inspiration-art-style-possibility-of-a-sequel-more/).
- Pixels are central to a 3D aesthetic — [PlayStation Blog postmortem, 2021-08-05](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/08/05/crafting-a-tiny-open-world-a-look-behind-the-scenes-at-the-creation-of-a-short-hike/).
- Palette sampled from autumn photographs of the Canadian Shield. Snippet only; which page holds this quote not confirmed.
- Wikipedia describes pixelated art with voxel and isometric graphics — [Wikipedia: A Short Hike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Short_Hike) (listed, not fetched).

**Smaller and rarer titles (from search; browser status not verified in play)**
- Island Artist: a hand-crafted world where you walk around making paintings; short and relaxing — [GamingOnLinux, 2021-03](https://gamingonlinux.com/2021/03/island-artist-is-a-short-and-sweet-game-about-relaxing-and-being-creative).
- Sketch: Woodlands: pen sketches scanned and coloured digitally — [zahni.itch.io](https://zahni.itch.io/woodlands).
- The Finding of Your Lingering Warmth: listed as a free HTML5 browser game tagged minimalist; no hand-painted detail given — [itch.io tag: minimalist](https://itch.io/misc/newest/released/tag-minimalist). Game-specific URL not confirmed.
- Candle (Teku Studios): hand-painted watercolour world, 2D puzzle adventure; not browser — [Hyperallergic](https://hyperallergic.com/candle-game-teku-studios.md).
- Short Trip: pencil-drawn; the player can change paper texture to shift mood; browser status unknown — [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/short-trip-impressions-review.md).

### Inferences
- Gris, Dorfromantik and Townscaper rely on constraint rules (a style guide, adjacency, flat shading) rather than detail. Those rules transfer to an archive interface better than any single look.
- Colour-as-chapter (Gris) is a navigation device: each cabinet "room" or era gets one key colour.
- No evidence that the main titles (Gris, Monument Valley, Dorfromantik, Florence, A Short Hike) run in a browser. Gris and Monument Valley are likely engine or mobile builds (memory, unverified).

## 2. Which websites show warm minimalism in cultural, museum or archive contexts?

### Takeaway
Warm, textured museum web design is mostly agency case studies and concept projects. The only ochre-in-museum example is a secondary description with no URL. No verified, live, textured, warm museum or archive site was found. Treat this area as a gap needing direct browsing.

### Cited Findings
- Musée d'Orsay (American Friends): the teal and light-blue brand palette was extended with neutrals and yellow ochre, a tone drawn from paintings of that era (snippet; source URL not returned by the search). The only ochre-in-museum case found.
- Chester Beatty website, designed by New Graphic (Diarmuid Slattery, David Garavin, Daniel Costello, Declan Behan): [100archive.com](https://www.100archive.com/projects/chester-beatty-website). Content not read; date unknown. Worth checking as a real cultural site.
- Nonprofit museum website: minimal design that carries over the museum's physical theme — [Excellent Web World](https://www.excellentwebworld.com/project/nonprofit-museum-website-design/). No mention of ochre or texture.
- Velora Art Museum (concept project): ivory and burgundy palette chosen to complement the artwork rather than compete with it — [Contra](https://contra.com/p/NZLgsloL-velora-art-museum-website-design). Concept, not a live site.
- Ludwig Museum brochure (print): background words set lightly to add texture — [Talyssa Cruz portfolio](https://talyssacruz.myportfolio.com/case-study-museum-ludwig-brochure). Print, not web.
- Listed but not read: [Tubik Lumen Museum](https://tubikstudio.com/blog/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/9521); [Melton Museum (Cuttlefish)](https://cuttlefish.com/our-work/melton-museum); [Gemeente Museum on DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com/best-design/gemeente-museum). Mainstream, probably low value.

### Inferences
- Warm museum web is mostly a palette choice (ivory, ochre, burgundy) with the artwork as the colour source. Transferable rule: derive the palette from the archive's own objects, not from a theme.

### Gaps
- No live, warm, textured museum or archive site verified. Needs direct browsing of museum sites, which was blocked here.
- Orsay ochre claim has no URL.

## 3. What design principles recur, and which are concrete enough to hand to an artist?

### Takeaway
Concrete sourced principles: a written style guide with fixed rules (Dorfromantik); colour as the structural and narrative key (Gris); flat shading and simple lighting for low cost (Townscaper); palette sampled from real photographs (A Short Hike). Texture work is sourced at the technique level (watercolour and pencil simulation, textured text, procedural texture). No sourced hex values, ratios, type pairings or easing curves were found.

### Cited Findings
- Style guide with rules for proportions, lighting and perspective, with a "simple but warm and playful" target — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/how-dorfromantik-expands-its-cozy-world-through-minimalist-design) (snippet).
- Colour chapters and colour as narrative — [PLITCH](https://blogs.plitch.com/en/blog/interview-with-nomada-studio) (snippet).
- Flat shading and simple lighting to reduce cost — Townscaper summary (see section 1; per-claim URL not confirmed).
- Palette sampled from autumn photographs of the Canadian Shield — A Short Hike (snippet).
- "As few pixels as possible" as a starting constraint — [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/interview-a-short-hike-dev-on-bringing-the-game-to-switch-inspiration-art-style-possibility-of-a-sequel-more/).
- Textured text via background-clip: Sara Soueidan, "Techniques for Creating Textured Text," dated 2013-12-02 — [Codrops author page](https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/author/sarasoueidan). The article itself was not fetched.
- Warm, natural aesthetics often need textures and patterns beyond HTML — [Creative Bloq](https://creativebloq.com/computer-arts/adding-texture-web-projects-11098961) (snippet; date not shown).
- WebGL oil-paint filter for images — [Framer marketplace: Oil Paint Cursor](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/components/oil-paint-cursor/) (listed, not read).

### Inferences
- A usable palette rule for an archive would be role-based, not hex-based: one paper ground, one ink, and two or three accents (ochre, clay, soft green), each tied to one archive category. This is my inference, not sourced.
- Texture is cheapest as procedural canvas or CSS, not image files, which keeps the asset budget low.

### Gaps
- No sourced hex values, colour ratios, type pairings or motion easing curves. The Codrops and Awwwards pages needed for these were not reachable.
- Spacing rules: no sourced figures.

## 4. Which examples are lightweight (browser, under about 1 MB of assets)?

### Takeaway
No main title was verified as a lightweight browser build. The lightweight leads are procedural-canvas tools and rule-based low-poly methods. Browser playability of Townscaper and the itch.io titles is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- Townscaper: low-poly with flat shading and simple lighting for weak hardware — Townscaper summary (section 1). A "Townscaper browser version" page exists in search results — [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=298540). Content not read; browser status unverified.
- Sapo.js: an interactive canvas procedural texture generator — [Google Experiments](https://experiments.withgoogle.com/sapojs) (listed, not read).
- Codrops demos on the canvas and background-clip tags — [Codrops hub](https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/tag/background-clip). The claim that demos are MIT-licensed is from a search summary and is unverified.
- Gris: likely an engine build (memory, unverified). Monument Valley and Dorfromantik: likely native or mobile (memory, unverified). No asset sizes sourced.

### Inferences
- The lightweight route is procedural: canvas paper grain, CSS background-clip text texture, flat-shaded low-poly 3D. This fits an asset budget under 1 MB.

### Gaps
- No verified asset size for any title.
- Townscaper browser build not verified.
- Four of the five itch.io/small-game browser claims rest on a single search result each.
